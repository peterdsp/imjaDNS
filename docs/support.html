Getting started

  • Open imjaDNS and complete the short onboarding walkthrough.
  • Browse providers by category (Privacy, Speed, Family Safe, Security, Gaming).
  • Tap a profile and choose Apply to activate it system-wide — no VPN required.
  • Confirm the DNS configuration when iOS prompts you in Settings.

Frequently asked questions

Does imjaDNS use a VPN?

No. imjaDNS applies DNS settings directly through Apple's NEDNSSettingsManager, so your traffic isn't routed through any tunnel or third-party server.

Are my DNS lookups encrypted?

Yes — every supported provider uses DNS over HTTPS (DoH) or DNS over TLS (DoT), so your queries stay private.

Can I add my own DNS server?

Absolutely. Create a custom profile with your own IPv4, IPv6, DoH URL, or DoT hostname. The app validates each field before saving.

Do you collect any of my data?

Never. imjaDNS performs no analytics, tracking, or logging. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I remove a DNS profile?

Open the active profile in the app and tap Remove, or delete the imjaDNS configuration under Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → DNS.
